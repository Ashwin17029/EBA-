What we do

Comprehensive financial services

From day-to-day books to long-term strategy, we cover every corner of your finances so you can focus on running your business.

01 — Tax

Tax Preparation & Planning

As Registered Tax Agents with the Fiji Revenue and Customs Service, we deliver accurate, on-time filing alongside proactive planning to keep your tax position efficient and compliant.

  • VAT, income tax & PAYE returns
  • FBT, SRL & STT lodgements
  • FRCS representation & audit support
  • Tax planning & advice
02 — Books

Bookkeeping

Clean, current, and reliable books every month so you always know exactly where your business stands.

  • Bank reconciliation & ledgers
  • Financial & management accounts
  • Accounts payable & receivable
  • MYOB setup & support
03 — Payroll

Payroll Management

Timely, compliant payroll that keeps your team paid and your statutory filings in order, without the headaches.

  • PAYE & FNPF filings
  • Payslips & records
  • Leave & entitlements

Business Consulting
